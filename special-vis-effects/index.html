How Movies Brought Magic to Life

Posted on October 21, 2026 by Gaebria Dunovan
All Posts

From the monstrous shark of Jaws (1975) to the creatures of Avatar (2009), visual and special effects (FX) have come a long way. Both practices date back to the late 19th century, but FX now versus then are practically in different worlds.

The 1902 French film, A Trip to the Moon, is widely considered to be a milestone in special effects, blowing audiences’ minds with what would now be considered rudimentary camera tricks and cheesily-painted backgrounds. So, how did we go from that to recreating the huge galaxies of space and giant creatures we used to only be able to dream of?
